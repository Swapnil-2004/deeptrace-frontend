How DeepTrace Works

Three stages of forensic analysis powered by boundary-topology science

01

Upload & Extract

MediaPipe detects the face and extracts 468 landmarks, isolating the boundary at hairline, jaw and ears — the exact regions where AI always fails.

02

BTD Analysis

9 forensic signals examine boundary curvature, chromatic aberration, sensor noise and facial symmetry. Temporal BTD tracks boundary evolution across video frames.

Result & Heatmap

EfficientNet-B4 and ViT scores combine with BTD forensics in a calibrated ensemble. Grad-CAM heatmap highlights the exact region that drove the decision.

Boundary-Topology Detector (BTD) — AI generation and face-swapping always struggle at topological boundaries: the hairline, ears and jaw. DeepTrace analyses these exact regions where the mathematics of generation breaks down, leaving forensic evidence that no post-processing can hide.

The Project

About DeepTrace

DeepTrace is a final year BTech project that pushes the state of deepfake detection through forensic signal analysis and boundary topology tracking. Built with the conviction that every AI-generated image leaves evidence — you just need to know where to look.

The Boundary-Topology Detector (BTD) algorithm forms the academic core of this work — examining the mathematical fingerprints that AI generation leaves at facial boundaries, especially under motion.
